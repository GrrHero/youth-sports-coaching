Week 1 · Session 2: Soccer Dribbling + Balance (25 min)

← Session 1 · Week 1 overview · Session 3 →

Warm-up: Body Part Simon Says with a Ball (4 min)

Each boy has a ball. "Simon says touch the ball with your knee... elbow... bottom!" Then "Simon says dribble... STOP the ball with your foot!" - Why: gets them touching the ball a lot, and they're listening without noticing.

Skill 1: Dribbling — "Small Touches" (8 min)

Break it down: 1. Walk it: Walk while tapping the ball with the inside or outside of the foot. The ball stays "on a leash," no more than a big step away. 2. Jog it: Same thing at a jog. 3. Stop it: On "red light," put the sole of the foot on top of the ball ("put the ball to sleep"). 4. Turn it: Pull it back with the sole or tap it around (7yo).

Cue words: "Small touches." "Ball on a leash." "Foot on top!" (for the stop).

Demo: Walk slowly with tiny taps, then do the "wrong" version: one big kick that sends the ball flying, then chase it. Ask: "Which way can I stop it faster?"

Common errors and fixes: | Error | Looks like | Fix | |---|---|---| | Big toe-kicks | Ball flies 15 ft, kid chases it | "Small touches, like the ball is a puppy on a leash." Make the area smaller | | Toe pokes | Ball goes wobbly or sideways | "Use your laces or the side of your foot, not your toe." Guided discovery: "Try toe, then side. Which goes straighter?" | | Head down the whole time | Runs into things | Hold up fingers: "How many fingers?" They have to look up to answer | | Can't stop it | Ball rolls away on red light | Practice standing still: "Tap, tap, foot on top." | | Only uses one foot | Fine for now | Leave it this week. Next week add "can you use both feet?" |

5yo vs. 7yo: The 5yo walks or jogs it, and stopping on command is a big win. The 7yo jogs and adds a pull-back turn on "U-turn!", and you can time how many crossings he makes in 30 seconds.

Fun: Red Light / Green Light / Yellow Light (see the Cal South video below). Green = fast dribble, yellow = slow, red = foot on top. Add "Purple light = dribble with your other foot" or "Honk your horn if you get close to another car!"

Water break (1–2 min)

Skill 2: Balance Challenges (6 min)

Break it down: Stand on one foot → one foot with eyes closed (7yo) → one foot while catching a soft toss → hopping. Cue words: "Be a statue." "Tummy tight." Demo: You stand on one foot and wobble on purpose, then "tighten your tummy" and get steady. Kids love seeing Dad wobble. Common errors: Looking down (fix: "Look at my hand" and hold it at eye level). Holding their breath or freezing up (fix: "Breathe like a balloon"). 5yo vs. 7yo: 5yo: arms out like an airplane, 5 seconds. 7yo: arms crossed, 10 sec, eyes closed for 3. Fun: "Flamingo Contest." Who can balance longest while Dad tries to make them laugh (no touching)? Count out loud.

Finale: Sharks and Minnows (5 min)

Each boy dribbles a ball (the minnows). Dad is the shark, with no ball, who tries to kick their balls out of the square. If you lose your ball, you get it back and keep going, so no one is eliminated. - 5yo: Dad is a slow "baby shark." - 7yo: Dad is a "great white" (a bit faster).

Huddle

"How did you keep the ball away from the shark?" (Guided discovery: they'll say "keep it close!")

Week 1 · Session 4: Flag Football Day (25 min)

← Session 3 · Week 1 overview · Session 5 →

Warm-up: Tail Tag (5 min)

Everyone tucks a sock or flag in a back pocket or waistband. Try to grab the others' tails while protecting your own, in a small area. - Why: builds dodging, change of direction and flag-pulling instincts before you've taught anything.

Skill 1: Flag Pulling — "Break Down, Grab, Rip" (8 min)

Break it down: 1. Eyes on the hips: Watch the belt (the hips), not the head or ball, because hips don't fake. 2. Break down: As you get close, take short "choppy" steps, bend the knees, and get low like a goalie. 3. Grab: Grab the flag near the belt. 4. Rip: Pull it straight down and away, then hold it up high and yell "FLAG!"

Cue words: "Eyes on the belt." "Chop, chop, GRAB." "Hold it up!"

Demo: Have the 7yo walk slowly past you wearing flags. Show the choppy steps and the low position, grab, rip, hold up. Then do the "wrong" version: run full speed, reach and miss. Ask: "Why did I miss?"

Common errors and fixes: | Error | Looks like | Fix | |---|---|---| | Diving or reaching early | Lunges and misses | "Chop, chop, then grab." Practice slowing down on a cone before the grab | | Grabbing the shirt or body | Holding, pushing | Clear rule: "Flags only. Grab the flag, not the friend." Restart if it happens | | Watching the ball or head | Gets faked out | "Eyes on the belt buckle" | | Running past the runner | Overshoots | Practice the "breakdown" (stop low like a goalie) on your whistle | | Pulls weakly, flag stays | Grabs but doesn't pull | "Rip it down like starting a lawnmower." |

5yo vs. 7yo: The 5yo pulls flags from Dad walking, then jogging slowly. The 7yo pulls from a jogging Dad who changes direction once. Try the Zig Zag drill from the NFL FLAG video: stationary "targets" with flags, and he runs to each, breaks down and pulls.

Fun: "Flag Collector." Dad wears 4–6 flags (socks in the waistband). The boys try to collect them all in 60 seconds. Count each boy's own total and try to beat it next round.

Skill 2: Running with the Ball + Simple Catch (6 min)

Break it down: 1. Carry: "High and tight." Ball tucked against the chest/side, hand covering the point ("hide the ball"). 2. Catch (big soft ball or junior football): Hands out, "make a diamond" with thumbs and pointers for chest-high balls, and "look it in." 3. Run with it: Catch, tuck, run to a cone.

Cue words: "Hide it!" "Diamond hands!"

Common errors: Carrying the ball like a loaf of bread, swinging it (fix: "Squeeze it so the shark can't take it," then poke at it gently to test). Catching with the body (fix for the 7yo: "hands first, then tuck." For the 5yo, body catches are fine this week).

5yo vs. 7yo: The 5yo uses a foam ball, short underhand tosses, and any catch counts. The 7yo uses a junior football and runs a simple "go to the cone and turn around" before the catch.

Finale: Mini Game (5 min)

Dad is quarterback for both teams. Each boy takes turns catching (or taking a handoff) and running for the "end zone" while the other boy tries to pull his flag. Switch every play. - Handicap: the 7yo starts farther back when the 5yo is the runner, or the 5yo gets "two-hand touch counts" instead of needing a flag pull.

Huddle

"How did you get close enough to grab the flag?" (Guided discovery: "slowing down!")

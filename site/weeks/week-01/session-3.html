Week 1 · Session 3: Catching + Tee Hitting (25–30 min)

← Session 2 · Week 1 overview · Session 4 →

Warm-up: Balloon or Beach Ball Keep-Up (4 min)

Keep a balloon or beach ball off the ground using hands. Count the hits. - Why: builds tracking ("eyes on the ball") with a slow object. Zero fear.

Skill 1: Catching — "Eyes on It, Fingers Up" (8 min)

Break it down: 1. Fear-free first: Use a newspaper ball, foam ball or tennis ball. Bare hands. 2. Ready position: Hands out in front, "show me your pancake" (open hands). 3. Above the waist: Fingers point up and thumbs come together ("make a diamond"). 4. Below the waist: Fingers point down and pinkies come together. 5. Glove (7yo): Same thing with the glove. "Watch it go into the glove," then cover it with the throwing hand ("close the alligator's mouth").

Cue words: "Eyes on it!" "Fingers up!" (for high balls), "Alligator!" (glove + cover).

Demo: You catch a soft toss from the other boy (or toss one up to yourself). Exaggerate watching it all the way in, then say "I watched it all the way into my hands."

Common errors and fixes: | Error | Looks like | Fix | |---|---|---| | Turning the head or closing the eyes | Flinching | Switch to a softer ball (newspaper or foam). "Tell me what color the ball is when it hits your hands" (or draw a smiley face on it and ask them to see it) | | Fingers pointing at the ball | Ball jams the fingers or bounces off the heel of the glove | "Fingers to the sky!" Practice bare hand, one hand, fingers up (DNA Sports video below) | | Trapping against the chest | Hugging the ball | OK for the 5yo for now. For the 7yo: "Catch it out in front, like your hands are a basket" | | Glove stays closed or flat | Ball bounces out | "Open the alligator's mouth" before the throw | | Stiff arms | Ball bounces off | "Soft hands, like catching an egg." Toss an actual water balloon outside for fun |

5yo vs. 7yo: - 5yo: Big foam ball or beach ball, two hands, gentle underhand tosses from about 5 ft. Celebrate every catch like it's the World Series. - 7yo: Tennis ball → glove. Underhand, then soft overhand from 10–15 ft. Mix glove side, middle and backhand side.

Fun: "Catch Ladder." Every catch = take one step back. A drop = one step forward. "How far can you get?" (Personal best each day.)

Skill 2: Tee Hitting — "Squish the Bug" (8 min)

This is a first taste only; the full swing breakdown comes next week. Break it down: 1. Stance: Feet wider than the shoulders, standing beside the tee. The tee is even with the belly button, about a bat length away (check by touching the bat to the far edge of the plate or tee). 2. Hands: Knuckles lined up (or just "hands together, glove hand on the bottom"). 3. Load: Bat back by the back shoulder ("bat on your shoulder, then lift it off"). 4. Swing: Rotate the back foot and "squish the bug" under the back toe as the hips turn. 5. Finish: Bat ends over the front shoulder.

Cue words: "Squish the bug." "Eyes on the ball."

Demo: Slow-motion swing, with an exaggerated back-foot squish. Then a "statue swing": freeze at the finish and say "Belly button faces the pitcher!"

Common errors and fixes: | Error | Looks like | Fix | |---|---|---| | Hands crossed/backward | Wrong hand on top | Quick check: "Glove hand on the bottom." Put a sticker on the knob hand | | Chopping down | Hits the tee, not the ball | Lower the tee slightly. "Swing flat, like cutting a table in half" | | Standing too close/far | Hits with the handle or the tip | Measure with the bat: "Touch the ball with the end of the bat. That's your spot" | | Back foot planted | All arms | "Squish the bug!" | | Eyes up early | Misses the ball | "Watch the bat hit the ball." A sticker on the ball to look at helps |

5yo vs. 7yo: The 5yo uses a big ball or whiffle ball on the tee with a light, short bat, and making contact is the win. The 7yo uses a regular ball and aims at targets ("hit it past the cone").

Fun: "Home Run Derby." Cones at different distances score 1, 2 or 3 points. The 5yo also hits a newspaper ball or a balloon off the tee for laughs (Schupak video below).

SAFETY: Only one person near the batter. The other boy stands behind a "safety line" (cone) well away from the swing. Make it a big rule from day 1: "Bat is a tool, not a toy."

Finale: Run the Bases (4 min)

Hit off the tee, then run to "first base" (a cone) while Dad fields and tries to tag the base. Running form cue: "Cheek to cheek!"

Huddle

"What helped you catch more balls today?"

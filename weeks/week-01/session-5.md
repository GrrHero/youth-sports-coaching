# Week 1 · Session 5: "Backyard Olympics" (Fun Friday, 25–30 min)

[← Session 4](session-4.md) · [Week 1 overview](README.md)

*Purpose: show off the week's skills in games, celebrate, and quietly see where each boy is. No new teaching. Just cue reminders.*

Run 5–6 stations of 3–4 min each, one boy at a time or both at once. Keep score on a whiteboard or piece of paper, but **score each boy against his own Monday baseline**, not against his brother.

1. **Flamingo Stand:** Longest one-foot balance (each foot). *Cue: "Statue!"*
2. **Sprint Race:** 20 yards, "cheek to cheek." Handicap the 7yo (he starts 5 yards back, or starts sitting down).
3. **Castle Knockdown:** Throwing at bottles/cups. *Cue: "Point, step, throw."*
4. **Catch Ladder:** How far back can you get? *Cue: "Eyes on it."*
5. **Dribble Gates:** Set up 4 "gates" (two cones each) and dribble through as many as possible in 45 sec. *Cue: "Small touches."*
6. **Flag Pull Challenge:** Pull Dad's flags in 30 sec.

**Medal ceremony:** Hand out "awards" for effort and character, not just results: "Never Gave Up Award," "Best Listener Award," "Biggest Improvement Award." Make sure each boy gets one, and that they're true.

**Why it works:** kids need to feel progress. A celebration day locks in "I'm getting better, and practice is fun," which is the whole goal of Week 1. It also shows you what to focus on next week.

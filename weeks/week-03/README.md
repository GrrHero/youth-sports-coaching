## Week Overview

**Theme: Receive and redirect.** Last week was about moving the body well: leaning starts, hip turns, shuffling, cutting and running routes. This week the boys learn to **take the ball in** (ground balls, fly balls, a soccer pass, a handoff) and then **send it somewhere on purpose** (a throw at a target, a shot, a pass, a sprint through the bag). Almost every sport works this way. Catch, control, then act.

**What carries over from Week 2:**

| Week 2 skill | Week 3 next step |
|---|---|
| Belly hip-turn throws + distance ladder | Throwing **at a target** with follow-through; **crow hop** intro for the 7yo |
| Shuffle & snag backhand, mirror shuffle | **Ready position** and **alligator grounders** (field it in front, two hands) |
| Big-to-little ball catching | **Fly balls**: balloon, then beach ball, then tennis ball, getting under it |
| Gate passing, cone slalom, soccer bowling | **First touch** (receiving) into space, **laces shooting**, **1v1 shielding** |
| Laces grip, basket catch, route running | **QB throwing motion**, **handoffs**, **evasion** (no flag-guarding), **backpedal** |
| Turnaround races, freeze cuts | Run **through** first base; **jump-and-stick landings**, **lateral bounds**, **reaction catches** |

**Three rules for the whole week:**
1. **Play first, talk second.** Get them moving inside 30 seconds. Say no more than 10 to 15 words before the first rep.
2. **One cue word per activity.** Use only that word while they're moving. Save longer explanations for water breaks, and only if they ask.
3. **Two levels, one activity.** Both boys always do the same game. The 7yo gets a harder version (farther, faster, moving) and the 5yo gets an easier one (closer, slower, bigger ball). Nobody sits out.

**A quick note on the silent demo:** for each activity you'll see how to *show* it without talking. Kids this age copy what they see far better than they follow what they hear. Do the move slowly 2 or 3 times, a bit exaggerated, then once at normal speed. Then say the cue word and point at them. That's the whole instruction.

## Week 4 Preview

- **Hitting from front toss, to a direction:** moving from tee and soft toss to aiming line drives up the middle or to the opposite field. The boys have learned the swing; now they control *where* the ball goes.
- **Field-and-fire to a base:** combining alligator grounders and the crow hop into one smooth move with a throw to a "first baseman" (you or the brother). This connects the two halves of this week's baseball session.
- **Soccer 1v1 dribble moves:** a simple fake and a cut-away move to get past a defender, building on this week's shielding (keeping the ball) toward beating someone with it.
- **Flag football: throwing to a moving receiver on a route:** joining the Phone throw with last week's routes, plus a defensive backpedal-and-break on the ball.
- **Athleticism: single-leg hops and reaction starts:** building on Whisper landings and Skater bounds, with hopscotch-style patterns and "start on a signal" sprints from different positions (lying down, sitting, facing backward).
- **Group coaching focus:** running a short scrimmage where the game itself does the teaching, and how to coach in-game with only cue words.

## Week 3 Cue-Word Cheat Sheet

| Cue Word | Session / Activity | What it means to the kid |
|---|---|---|
| **Popcorn** | S1 Warm-up: Popcorn Ready | "Pop into my ready shape: feet wide, bottom down, hands out." |
| **Chomp** | S1 Skill: Alligator Grounders | "Bottom jaw (glove) on the grass, top jaw (hand) closes on the ball." |
| **Scoot** | S1 Skill: Crow Hop Target Throws | "Little hop, back foot sideways, then step and throw at the target." |
| **Middle** | S1 Game: Grounder Goalie | "Get my body in the middle of where the ball is rolling." |
| **Float** | S1 Cool-down: Glove Boat | "Breathe slow so my glove floats up and down." |
| **Piano** | S2 Warm-up: Piano Toe Taps | "Light, quick taps on top of the ball, left–right." |
| **Pillow** | S2 Skill: Pillow First Touch | "Soft foot. Catch the ball so it stays close (or goes to the gate)." |
| **Shoelaces** | S2 Skill: Shoelace Shots | "Hit the ball with my shoelaces, toe pointing down." |
| **Shell** | S2 Game: Treasure Keeper | "My body is a shell between the pirate and the ball." |
| **Sleepy** | S2 Cool-down: Sleepy Sole Rolls | "Roll the ball super slow." |
| **Moonwalk** | S3 Warm-up: Moonwalk Backpedal | "Small quick steps backward, chest forward." |
| **Window** | S3 Skill: Window Handoffs | "Near elbow up, other arm under. Make a window for the ball." |

| **Zigzag** | S3 Game: Mr. Wolf Flag Escape | "Change direction to get away. Feet, not hands." |

| **Pounce** | S4 Game: Pounce | "Explode to the ball and catch it after one bounce." |
| **Sloth** | S4 Cool-down: Sloth Crawl Tunnel | "Super slow crawl, opposite hand and knee." |
| **Orbit** | S5 Warm-up: Orbit Ball Wraps | "Circle the ball around my body like a moon." |
| **Umbrella** | S5 Skill: Umbrella Fly Balls | "Get under it, hands up above my eyes like an umbrella." |

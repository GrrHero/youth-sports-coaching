## Week Overview

**Theme: Two players, one play.** Last week every activity had a target or an opponent to read. This week the target is a *teammate*. A first baseman has to finish the play his brother starts. A soccer pass only works if the passer runs to get it back. A QB on the move has to square up to a receiver who is also moving, and a defender has to run to where the ball carrier is *going*. Even the athleticism day adds a partner rope game and a first taste of basketball ball handling. Basketball and soccer may be coming up for the boys, so this week sneaks in some light basketball-friendly skills (dribble, chest and bounce pass, pivot) without turning it into a basketball week.

**What carries over from Week 4:**

| Week 4 skill | Week 5 next step |
|---|---|
| Field-and-fire to first (Tick-tock), Beat the Runner | **Playing first base**: foot on the bag, stretch to the throw, and **glove-down tags** at a base |
| Laser Gate front toss, Pumpkin spray hitting | **Short coach pitch** for the 7yo (front toss for the 5yo), still aiming at gates, now timing a real pitching motion |
| 1v1 moves: the Trick and the Slice | **2v1 and the give-and-go (one-two)**: when to dribble, when to pass |
| Lead passes to a landing spot | **QB on the move**: rolling out and throwing while moving |
| Backpedal and break on the ball | **Pursuit angles**: run to the meeting point and pull the flag from the front, not chase from behind |
| Hopscotch patterns, Pogo bounces | **Chalk ladder footwork** and **first jump rope** practice |
| (new) | **Basketball-friendly ball skills**: fingertip dribble, chest and bounce passes, the pivot |

**Three rules for the whole week:**
1. **Teach both jobs.** Every partner skill has a giver and a getter (thrower and first baseman, passer and runner, QB and receiver). Teach the *getter's* job first, since that's the one kids forget. Then swap jobs every few reps so each boy feels both sides.
2. **One cue word per activity.** Same as always: only that word while they're moving. Say it *before* the rep. If you catch yourself saying a sentence, stop and say the word.
3. **One level each.** Each session lists one level for the 5yo and one for the 7yo. Start them there. Move up after about 4 successes out of 5; move down after 3 misses in a row. Change the setup quietly (distance, speed, ball size). No discussion.

**How to teach this week (whole-part-whole).** Teaching is hard because you already *see* the whole play in your head, and the boys don't yet. Instead of building up from tiny pieces, try this three-step sandwich:
1. **Whole (2 minutes):** play a simple version of the real thing right away, even if it's messy. Example: a 2v1 against you before any passing drill. Watch for the *one* thing that breaks down most.
2. **Part (4 to 6 minutes):** pull out just that one piece and practice it with a silent demo and one cue word. Example: the give-and-go against a cone.
3. **Whole again (the game):** put it right back into the game. Now they have a reason to use the part, and you'll see it show up.

The skill-then-game order in each session already follows this. The new habit is the quick "whole" at the start of Skill Work A: 1 or 2 minutes of the real thing before you teach. You learn what to fix, and they learn why the drill matters.

**New this week, effort-praise scripts:** with partner skills, praise the *job*, not the kid's talent. Swap "You're a natural first baseman!" for "You kept your foot on the bag and stretched. That's why it was an out." Swap "Good job" for "You ran to get it back after your pass. That's a one-two!" Specific effort praise ("you did X, and that's why Y happened") makes kids keep trying when things get hard; talent praise ("you're so good") makes them avoid hard things so they don't stop looking good.

## Week 6 Preview

Week 6 (Nov 9–15) is the last week of fall ball, so it's a "season finale" week: review, celebrate and show off, plus a peek ahead to winter sports.
- **Baseball: baserunning reads and calling the ball:** running on a ground ball versus holding on a fly ball, rounding first on a hit to the outfield, and calling "Mine!" on pop-ups so two fielders don't collide.
- **Soccer: shooting under pressure and a first goalie try:** shooting quickly after a one-two, plus basic goalkeeping (ready stance, catching a rolled ball, diving onto a soft ball on grass) for both boys.
- **Flag football: fakes and reverses:** a fake handoff and a simple reverse play, plus defenders reading the fake.
- **Athleticism: jump rope streaks and a basketball footwork intro:** rope streak challenges building on this week, and layup footwork ("right, left, up") at a lowered hoop or a laundry basket on a chair.
- **Session 5: "Goonies Showcase Day":** each boy picks his favorite drills from the six weeks and teaches one to Dad (a full teach-back).
- **Group coaching focus:** running the final game day and the awards moment smoothly, keeping kids engaged in a long season-ending game, and an off-season home plan to send parents.
